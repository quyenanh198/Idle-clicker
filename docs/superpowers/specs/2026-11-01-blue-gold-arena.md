# Blue and gold arena redesign

User selected the reference-video direction: deep blue UI, gold highlights and a battle-focused composition. Replace the rejected pastel treatment completely. Keep the previous constraints: a desktop frame that follows the screen, no introduction columns, and touch-friendly mobile play.

Use one continuous desert arena. The mission/wave HUD sits at the top, gears float centrally above the combat lane, and a compact royal-blue recruitment tray sits at the bottom. The tray disappears during combat in favor of a small speed/reward HUD. Avoid the previous separate side-by-side battlefield/engine panels.

Visual direction: saturated royal blue and navy, outlined white display text, beveled gold and steel gears, green power glow, chunky green Fight button, gold active navigation. Redraw fighters as small bold-outlined cartoon warriors and towers as compact battle forts. Sharper canyon scenery and warm orange battle sand replace the soft pastel background. Restyle Skills, Deck and Summon to the same blue/gold language.

Preserve simulation, card progression, saves, accessibility and pointer input. Validate a single full-width arena, board/shop separation on short windows, mobile touch drags, keyboard actions, combat and canvas tap mapping. Inspect desktop, mobile, deck, skills and battle screenshots before delivering the reviewable design.
